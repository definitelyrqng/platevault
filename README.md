# 🪪 PlateVault

**A community archive for license plate spotters.**

PlateVault is where spotters come to upload, browse, and collect license plates from around the world. Find a plate you've never seen before? Snap it, tag it, and add it to the archive forever.

---

## What is PlateVault?

License plates are more than numbers — they carry country codes, regional formats, special series, and history. PlateVault is a community built around documenting them. Every spot uploaded contributes to a growing, searchable archive organized by country.

Whether you're a casual spotter who noticed a foreign plate in a parking lot, or someone who actively hunts rare formats, PlateVault gives your finds a permanent home.

---

## What you can do

- **Upload a spot** — Photo + plate text + car details (brand, model, generation, trim, colour)
- **Browse by country** — Every country has its own gallery with all archived plates
- **Like & comment** — React to other spotters' finds
- **Multi-spots** — Already archived? It still counts. You'll see every sighting of the same plate together, and the original spotter gets notified
- **Get notified** — Likes, comments, multi-spots, and system messages all land in your notification inbox
- **Leaderboards** — See who's spotted the most, per country and globally
- **Profiles** — Your own page showing your archive, stats, and activity
